How it decides · click each check

Four money checks every title must pass.

▸
Efficiency

Cost per Viewing Hour

What we pay, divided by hours watched. Too high → overpaying per hour. Fixable by a lower price.

▸
Return

LTV-to-Cost multiple

Value created vs. what it costs. Must clear a floor. Fixable by a lower price.

▸
Stickiness

Churn mitigation

Does it stop subscribers cancelling? Must clear a hurdle. Not fixable by price. A fail here forces PASS.

▸
Speed

Payback horizon

How many months until it pays for itself. Fixable by a lower price.

Three are about price. One is about the title itself. That’s the whole secret to the three verdicts.

Small, transparent models learn each demand signal (viewing hours, retention) from open data and show their work, feeding the engine without ever being the gate. Optional comps, franchise-lineage, momentum, and market drivers can sharpen those inputs, each switched off by default.
