Station 3 · how it decides · click each

Four money checks every title must pass.

▸
Efficiency

Cost per Viewing Hour

What we pay, per hour actually watched. Too high → overpaying. Fixable by a lower price.

▸
Return

LTV-to-Cost multiple

Value created per dollar spent, must clear a 3.2× floor. Fixable by a lower price.

▸
Stickiness

Churn mitigation

Does it keep subscribers from cancelling? Must clear 1.5%. Not fixable by price, a fail here forces PASS.

▸
Speed

Payback horizon

Months until it pays for itself, must land within 12. Fixable by a lower price.

Three are about price, one is about the title itself. That split is the whole logic behind the three verdicts.

Station 4 · keeps itself honest · click each

It grades its own homework.

Enter what actually happened and it scores predicted vs reality, then tunes up when it drifts, without ever rewriting the past.

▸
↻

Backtest

Record real viewing hours and churn for a title; it reports accuracy as one clear error number (MAPE) with a predicted-vs-actual chart.

▸
⚒

📦

Version catalog

Every model version is listed, the active one flagged, any prior one rollback-able, so a result from last quarter stays reproducible under its own version.
